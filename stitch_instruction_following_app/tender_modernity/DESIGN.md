## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an intimate, sincere, and modern mobile-first confession experience. Rather than leaning into kitsch, oversized teddy bears, or cliché greeting-card graphics, the aesthetic takes inspiration from contemporary editorial design and hyper-refined product software: pristine typography, intentional negative space, gentle tactile surfaces, and whisper-soft warmth.

The brand persona is thoughtful, vulnerable, confident, and understated. The target audience values modern digital tastefulness—clean product design where every pixel breathes respect and genuine affection.

### Visual Style
- **Core Aesthetic:** Warm Modern Minimalism with Soft Editorial Nuance.
- **Atmosphere:** Clean, sunlit, and uncluttered. Surfaces lean warm-neutral with faint rose undertones, preventing cold clinical sterility while strictly avoiding childish tropes.
- **Focal Discipline:** Typography carries 90% of the emotional weight. Color acts as an emotional punctuation point rather than a decorative wash.

## Colors

The palette revolves around a single, decisive emotional accent—`#E11D48` (Rose 600)—paired with warm off-white canvas tones and deep stone neutrals.

### Usage Guidance
- **Canvas & Backing (`#FCFAF9`):** A custom warm off-white with an invisible hint of rose blush. It creates high contrast with typography without the starkness of pure digital white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Primary Accent (`#E11D48`):** Reserved strictly for primary call-to-actions (e.g., the "Buka" and "Iya" triggers), active states, and focal celebratory moments. It is never diluted into gradient washes across screen backgrounds.
- **Card & Elevated Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white for modular cards (such as the admin recap and response displays) to create clean separation against the canvas.
- **Borders (`#F3E8E8` / `#E4DCDC`):** Ultralight warm hairline borders to frame cards and outline buttons without visual noise.
- **Typography Tone (`#1C1917`):** Warm charcoal black ensures peak legibility with an organic, human touch.

## Typography

Typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** throughout all interfaces. Its balanced geometric construction, wide apertures, and subtly softened joints lend warm digital approachability while maintaining impeccable modern structure.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Question Headline:** Set in bold with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`). The proposal question ("Maukah kamu jadi pacarku?") should feel direct, clear, and confident.
- **Narrative Subtitles:** Kept short and quiet using `body-md` in `#78716C`, maintaining generous vertical offset from the headline.
- **Action Buttons:** Standardized to `label-lg` with medium-high weight for effortless tap legibility.
- **Avoid:** Any script, italic cursive, or juvenile display faces. Sincerity comes from clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built with a strictly **mobile-first, single-column container** model.

### Layout Rules
- **Viewport Anchor:** Mobile screen frames run at a default viewport width of `390px`. On desktop screens, the application is locked within a centered mobile viewport container (`max-width: 420px`), wrapped in a neutral backdrop to preserve the intimacy of a phone interaction.
- **Vertical Centering:** Flow screens (Landing, Question, Results) center content vertically (`min-height: 100dvh`) with deliberate vertical distribution:
  - Top padding ensures safe zone clearance (`unit-12`).
  - The central interaction cluster holds the message and response controls.
  - Bottom clearance cushions pill buttons safely above OS home bars.
- **Negative Space Rhythm:** Use `unit-8` (32px) or `unit-10` (40px) between headlines and interactive buttons to provide emotional room for contemplation. Avoid clustering elements together.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, skeuomorphic, or dramatic dark drop shadows, leaning instead into **whisper-soft ambient diffusion** and **crisp surface boundaries**.

- **Ambient Flat Surfaces:** Full-screen views (Landing, Question, Yes/No screens) live flat on the warm blush canvas `#FCFAF9` to feel tactile and printed rather than simulated 3D.
- **Card Separation (Admin & Recap):** When cards are introduced on utility pages, use surface contrast (`#FFFFFF` on `#FCFAF9`) paired with a micro-border `1px solid #F3E8E8` and an ethereal, low-contrast shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.03), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.02);`
- **Button Lift:** Primary CTA buttons use a delicate, tinted glow rather than a harsh black drop shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px 0 rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.22);`
  On active tap state, this compresses to `scale(0.98)` with reduced shadow spread.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Level 3 (Pill-shaped)** rounding strategy for touch targets and high-tier roundedness for structural cards.

- **Buttons & Input Controls:** Strict full-pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`). This removes sharp tension, making every action feel inviting, gentle, and tactile.
- **Containers & Modal Cards:** Softened with a generous `24px` radius (`rounded-xl`), creating an organic silhouette that pairs naturally with the pill buttons.
- **Tags & Badges:** Micro-pills with full rounding and clean internal horizontal padding.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill Action ("Buka", "Iya"):**
  - Background: `#E11D48`
  - Text: `#FFFFFF`, `label-lg`, center-aligned
  - Height: `54px`, full width or minimum width `160px`
  - Radius: `9999px` (full pill)
  - Shadow: `0 4px 14px 0 rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.22)`
  - Interaction: Subtly scales to `0.98` with crisp 150ms ease-out transitions.
- **Secondary Ghost/Outline Button ("Nggak"):**
  - Background: `transparent` (or `#FFFFFF` on noisy contexts)
  - Border: `1px solid #E4DCDC`
  - Text: `#78716C`, `label-lg`
  - Height: `54px`, matched to primary
  - Radius: `9999px`
  - Interaction: Hover state transitions to text `#1C1917` and border `#A8A29E`.
- **Close/Exit Button (Result Screens):**
  - Transparent pill or subtle filled pill (`#FFFFFF` with `border: 1px solid #F3E8E8`), `label-md`, anchored toward the bottom.

### Cards (Admin / Recap Screen)
- **Container:**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid #F3E8E8`
  - Radius: `20px`
  - Padding: `24px`
- **Content Row:**
  - Label: `label-mono` in `#A8A29E`, uppercase
  - Value: `headline-md` in `#1C1917` or `#E11D48`
  - Timestamp: `body-sm` in `#78716C`

### Text Fields (Admin Login)
- **Container:**
  - Height: `50px`
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid #E4DCDC`
  - Radius: `9999px`
  - Padding: `0 20px`
  - Font: `body-md`
  - Focus Ring: `1px solid #E11D48` with soft ambient glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.1)`.

### Animation Canvases & Canvas Placeholders
- **Screen 3 (Yes Screen):** Full-screen overlay area dedicated to joyful micro-confetti or crisp rose-tinted heart burst particles, floating out smoothly behind the central "Yeay!" confirmation badge.
- **Screen 4 (No Screen):** Quiet, slow-tempo descending muted particles (`#A8A29E`, 40% opacity) evoking gentle autumn leaves or calm snow; minimal, dignified, never dramatic or mocking.
